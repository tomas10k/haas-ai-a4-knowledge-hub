# DFMW Design Process

## Summary

The DFMW design process is a four-step procedure that outlines how design concepts are executed. This process involves framing, estimating, building, testing, and finally validating and communicating the design.

## Key points

- The four-step design process includes Frame and Sketch, Estimate and Build, Stress Test and Iterate, and Validate, Socialize and Communicate.
- Frame and Sketch involves determining the audience and the intention, and using simplifying formulas.
- Estimate and Build requires estimating and ballparking, choosing tools, and following best practices.
- Stress Test and Iterate involves considering scenarios, testing the model, and building sensitivity analysis.
- Validate, Socialize and Communicate involves checking for accuracy, soliciting feedback, and delivering with impact.
- Class 1 covered Frame and Sketch and Estimate.
- Class 2 focused on Build, which included the style guide, visual hierarchy, and fast formatting tools.
