# Good Model Design

## Summary

Good model design is determined by three factors: the context of who uses the model and for what purpose, the domain-specific guidelines for good design, and the results of testing. This process helps ensure the model functions correctly.

## Key points

- Good design is evaluated based on Context, which is who uses the model and for what purpose.
- Good design is evaluated based on Principles, which are domain specific guidelines for good design.
- Good design is evaluated based on Testing, such as the ZipCar scavenger hunt, where several models reached the correct answer with different design choices.
- Two of the models in the ZipCar scavenger hunt were built with AI.
- A guiding quote from John Gall in *Systemantics* states that a complex system that works has invariably evolved from a simple system that worked, suggesting designing small sections first.
