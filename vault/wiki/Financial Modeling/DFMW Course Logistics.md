# DFMW Course Logistics

## Summary

This section outlines the logistics for the course, detailing the structure of homework assignments and the options for the final deliverable. Understanding these logistics is important for completing the course requirements and preparing for the final assessment.

## Key points

- Homework runs on two tracks: Part A reinforces weekly concepts, and Part B builds a personal model.
- Part A homework includes tasks such as building two small models, covering a housing case and a unit economics case with a partner, revising a one tab model, and building a multi tab cash burn model with a partner.
- Part B homework involves framing and sketching, researching inputs and assumptions, drafting one tab, and revising it after feedback.
- The final deliverable offers two options: an "Excel Expert" final quiz or an "AI Modeler" final project.
- The "Excel Expert" option is an individual, case-based quiz covering Classes 1 to 6, taken in class on October 14 through Honorlock.
- The "AI Modeler" option is a team of 1 or 2, requiring a 5 to 7 tab model, a 4 to 5 page reflection paper, and a 15 minute presentation with the instructor, due October 14 at 11:59 pm.
