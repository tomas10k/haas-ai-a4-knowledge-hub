# Frame and Sketch

## Summary

The Frame and Sketch stage involves framing questions to define the scope and audience for a model. This stage is crucial because the output, a short model brief, launches subsequent work like the Sketch and Estimate, whether done by oneself or with AI.

## Key points

- Framing questions require defining the Audience and the decision being made.
- The Scope must be considered to avoid overbuilding.
- The default starting point for calculation is Profit = Revenue − Variable Costs − Fixed Costs.
- AI can pressure test a drafted brief by surfacing missed stakeholder perspectives or tightening vague language.
- Useful framing prompts include pressure testing the brief's question, understanding audience needs, and considering what a key decision-maker wants to see first.
