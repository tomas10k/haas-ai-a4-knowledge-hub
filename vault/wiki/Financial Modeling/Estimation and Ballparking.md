# Estimation and Ballparking

## Summary

This stage involves estimating or ballparking before building a model, which requires creating a structure sketch, a check figure, and a list of key assumptions. The check figure should be within about 20% of the exact answer and serves to catch errors in the model construction.

## Key points

- The check figure should be within about 20% of the exact answer; it is not the final answer produced by the spreadsheet.
- The six-step estimation process includes sketching the structure, identifying inputs, computing in chunks, rounding to compatible numbers, annualizing or aggregating, and performing a sanity check.
- Strategic rounding involves picking numbers that make the next operation easy, using friendly numbers like 5, 10, 20, 25, 50, 100, 200, 500, 1,000.
- Offset the rounding by rounding one factor up and the other down to prevent errors from compounding.
- Common mistakes include forgetting to annualize figures, mixing up time periods, adding instead of multiplying, dropping or adding a zero, and double negatives.
- Unit conversions for annualization include multiplying per hour by 2,000, per day by 365, per week by 52, per month by 12, and per quarter by 4.
