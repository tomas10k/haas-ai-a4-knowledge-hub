# Modeling Formulas and Frameworks

## Summary

This section outlines various formulas and frameworks used across different business areas, including revenue calculation, profit metrics, working capital calculations, valuation methods, customer and SaaS metrics, funnel analysis, risk assessment, platform revenue, and strategy frameworks. These tools are intended to generate ideas rather than serve as definitive lists.

## Key points

- Revenue formulas include definitions for multi stream revenue, recurring revenue, capacity based revenue, advertising, and cohort revenue.
- Profit and unit economics metrics include contribution margin, gross margin, operating margin, net margin, breakeven units, breakeven revenue, operating leverage, ROI, and ROIC.
- Operating models include formulas for profit, cash runway, EBITDA, payback period, and the "corkscrew" for accumulating balances.
- Working capital metrics include DSO, DIO, DPO, and the cash conversion cycle.
- Valuation metrics include NPV, IRR, free cash flow, DCF, and multiples like EV.
- Customer and SaaS metrics include customer lifetime value, CAC, CAC payback, churn, ARPU, net dollar retention, the Rule of 40, and the SaaS quick ratio.
- Funnel metrics include conversion rate, click through rate, AARRR stages, and NPS.
- Risk and uncertainty tools include expected value, scenario analysis, sensitivity analysis, and Monte Carlo simulation.
- Platform metrics include marketplace revenue, network effects, and creator economy calculations.
- Strategy frameworks include Porter's Five Forces, Business Model Canvas, Ansoff Matrix, SWOT, BCG Matrix, and 3Cs.
