## Summary

This section details the case example of The Kitchen at Clark Street, which is a running case in Class 1. It highlights the operational aspects, ownership, and revenue assumptions related to the restaurant's performance.

## Key points

- The restaurant has 46 seats and serves two dinner turns each night.

- The P&L is barely breaking even after a year of doing nearly everything right.
- Players include Carmine ("Carm") Russo, co owner and head chef; Natalie ("Nat"), co owner running front of house; and Uncle Jimmy, the lead investor.
