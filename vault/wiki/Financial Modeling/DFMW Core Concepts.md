# DFMW Core Concepts

## Summary

The four DFMW core concepts define what every model should address. These concepts ensure that a model effectively covers the audience, the underlying business story, the tools used, and the quality of the model itself.

## Key points

- Every model should answer the question: who is this for and what question is being examined.
- The model must address the underlying business story.
- The model should describe how tools bring the thinking to life.
- The model must address the quality of the model, ensuring it is accurate, transparent, and empowering.
