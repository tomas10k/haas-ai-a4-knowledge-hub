# Responding to First Offers

## Summary

This section outlines strategies for responding to a first offer, distinguishing between extreme and reasonable offers. The goal is to manage the negotiation dynamic by controlling the discussion and avoiding pitfalls like the winner's curse.

## Key points

- For an extreme first offer, talk about your resistance point, not their aspiration point.
- Do not counteranchor with an equally extreme number, as this leaves both sides meeting in the middle of an artificial range.
- Take back the power with a question, such as "Unfortunately, I can never do that. Do you still want to talk?" or "Let's talk about a package that would work for both of us."
- Wipe the offer off the table, then present your own justified package or stay silent and let them negotiate with themselves.
- For a reasonable first offer, do not ask where it came from and do not express disgust or incredulity.
- Do not immediately counter; deflect first.
- Do not immediately accept to avoid the winner's curse, which is the feeling that you left value on the table because the offer was accepted too easily.
