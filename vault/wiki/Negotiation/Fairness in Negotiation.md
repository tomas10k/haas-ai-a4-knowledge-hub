# Fairness in Negotiation

## Summary

Fairness in negotiation involves various concepts related to splitting resources and judging processes. It is important to understand different definitions of fairness and how to structure negotiations to ensure both parties feel respected and that the outcome is perceived as equitable.

## Key points

- The ultimatum game involves one player proposing how to split $10 in a single, final offer, where rejection leaves both with nothing.
- Three kinds of fairness are equality (equal shares), equity (shares proportional to contribution), and need (shares based on need).
- Procedural justice is judged by whether people were respected and heard through checking in ("Does this seem fair to you?") and listening actively ("Can you tell me your perspective on this?").
- When negotiating, it is important to understand how each party defines fair, frame your preferred outcome so it feels fair to the counterpart, and make the process itself feel fair.
