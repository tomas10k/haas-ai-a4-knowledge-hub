# First Offers

## Summary

Making the first offer is important because it anchors the outcome, as first offers and final deals are strongly correlated. Justifying the offer can increase compliance and make the other party more likely to adjust.

## Key points

- First offers and final deals were strongly correlated (r = 0.60, p < .001, R² about 0.36) in in-class data.
- First offers should be ambitious enough, optimistic but realistic.
- Present a package, not a single number, and never a range.
- Do not make the offer immediately; build rapport and check assumptions first.
- Justify the offer to raise compliance, as a justified offer seems more serious and makes the other party more likely to adjust.
- Do not make the first offer in salary negotiations or when you know less than the other party; invite them to name their price.
