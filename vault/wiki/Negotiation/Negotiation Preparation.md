# Negotiation Preparation

## Summary

The preparation sheet covers essential elements for negotiation, including defining goals, understanding value creation, and assessing various strategic components. This preparation is important because it guides the negotiation process by clarifying objectives, identifying leverage points, and considering alternatives.

## Key points

- The course prep sheet covers Goals and interests (the purpose behind what you want)
- It covers Value creating options
- It covers Aspiration point package and resistance point package
- It covers BATNA
- It covers Sources of power
- It covers Comparables, meaning objective criteria
- It covers Strategy, including how to build rapport
- Social norms and other people's projections shape aspirations (Correll, 2004), so check whether your goals are your own.
