# Giving Feedback

## Summary

Giving feedback after practice negotiations is a part of the learning process. The goal is to motivate rather than demoralize by focusing on areas for improvement. Feedback should be real, honest, direct, and helpful.

## Key points

- Feedback is part of the learning after each practice negotiation.
- Motivate rather than demoralize.
- Focus on something that can improve.
- Be real, honest, direct, and helpful.
- Look for the common thread in feedback over several weeks rather than overreacting to one session.
