# Building Rapport

## Summary

Building rapport involves starting with informal conversation on personal or social topics rather than jumping straight to the point. This approach is important because a rapport group was more likely to reach a deal in an experiment where people were randomly assigned to build rapport first.

## Key points

- Start with informal conversation ("schmoozing") on personal or social topics.
- The rapport group was more likely to reach a deal in an experiment where people were randomly assigned to build rapport first.
- Conversation is a learnable skill.
- Before a conversation, prepare bullet points on topics to explore, such as what you know about the person, what they said last time, or their company or public profile.
- The book *TALK* by Alison Wood Brooks covers this skill in depth.
