# What a Negotiation Is

## Summary

A negotiation is a process where parties share information and use problem-solving to reach solutions that benefit both sides. This process is important because it helps in achieving successful outcomes, preserving reputation, and expanding value for future interactions.

## Key points

- A negotiation is a process by which parties share information and use problem solving to achieve mutually beneficial solutions.
- The counterpart is more likely to agree and follow through and implement the deal.
- Your reputation is preserved for future deals.
- Mutual benefit expands the pie and creates value.
- A successful outcome means both parties feel they won, there is repeat business, and you keep a good reputation.
