# Distributive and Integrative Negotiation

## Summary

This section compares distributive and integrative negotiation styles, noting that integrative negotiation is the focus of the course. Integrative negotiation requires skills in creating and claiming value.

## Key points

- Distributive negotiation has a short term time horizon and aims for a win or lose result.
- Integrative negotiation has a long term time horizon and aims for a win win result.
- Integrative negotiation focuses on joint gain rather than individual gain.
- Integrative negotiation involves congruent interests and multiple issues.
- Integrative negotiation involves issues where sides have different priorities, allowing for trade.
- Pareto improvement is a change that makes at least one party better off without making anyone worse off.
- Pareto optimal points form the Pareto frontier, where no one can be made better off without making someone else worse off.
- Sandbagging is a feigned concession used to extract a concession elsewhere, which is considered suboptimal.
